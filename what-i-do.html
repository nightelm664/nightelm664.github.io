Process

What I actually do

An honest loop from brief to report — the same shape whether it’s a Race to World First partner reel, a Discord drop, or a brand activation on the road.

  1. Discovery & brief

    Start with the audience, the moment, and the constraint. For Echo that might be a Mythic kill window or a partner brief from AMD or Corsair. For personal content it’s cultural timing — WoW Forever launch, a meme format, a joke the community is already halfway toward. Clarify the goal: awareness, engagement, partner delivery, or community payoff.

  2. Content strategy

    Map formats to platforms — Instagram Reels, X quote posts, Discord, short-form highlight packages. Decide what is campaign architecture versus reactive race content. Protect brand voice while staying gaming-native: humour that lands with players, not corporate “gamer speak.”

  3. Film, edit, create

    Hands-on video: planning, filming and editing for organic and partner deliverables. Day-in-the-life partner reels, kill announcements, wrap-ups, nostalgia memes, humour clips with tagged talent. Ship on race time — speed without looking rushed.

  4. Talent & partners

    Scout and coordinate influencers, creators and players. Integrate sponsors into the race narrative (Corsair Scimitar, AMD day-in-life) so the product feels native to the story. Keep talent comfortable on camera and on-message without killing personality.

  5. Community — Discord & live

    Day-to-day community at scale (GIANTX Discord at 28,500 members from CV). Live event presence at Gamescom, Insomnia and Dreamhack. Discord isn’t a broadcast channel — it’s where culture sticks between the posts.

  6. Reporting & optimisation

    Track what actually moved — views, likes, comments, comment-to-like ratio as an algorithm signal, follower multiples. Feed patterns back into the next brief: nostalgia + timing on personal; real-time world-first and end-of-tier emotion on org accounts; partner reels as proof even when raw reach is lower.
