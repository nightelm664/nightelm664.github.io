Challenge

Multi-title esports org social is a different sport from a single race campaign. Day-to-day publishing across platforms, a large Discord, influencer coordination, brand partners with different risk appetites, and live events that don’t wait for your content calendar. The work had to stay coherent while titles, rosters and partners moved.

Approach

As Social Media Specialist at GIANTX (formerly EXCEL), owned day-to-day social across major platforms and a Discord community of 28,500 members (from CV). Influencer and talent scouting & coordination; performance tracking and campaign strategy; freelancer oversight. Brand campaigns with JD Sports, EE, Just Eat and HSBC. Live presence at Gamescom, Insomnia and Dreamhack.

Standout programmes included educational platform content for GX League of Legends and the EE Power Up Programme supporting women in UK gaming.

Community at scale, brand campaigns that respect gaming audiences, and live events that ship content on the day.

Outcome

All figures below are from CV — labelled clearly; no fresh post scrapes attached to this page.

  • Discord: 28,500-member community under day-to-day social & community remit.
  • GX League of Legends: 167k net audience growth from educational platform content.
  • EE Power Up Programme: ESA Award-winning programme supporting women in UK gaming.
  • JD Sports: international gaming activation tour across Europe.
  • Brand delivery: campaign work with EE, HSBC and Just Eat alongside JD Sports.
  • Live events: Gamescom, Insomnia, Dreamhack content and activations.

Why it worked

  • Discord-scale community gives a home base between match days and campaigns.
  • Educational LoL content proved growth can come from usefulness, not only hype.
  • Award-winning EE Power Up shows brand work with social purpose, not only product pushes.
  • On-the-ground Europe tour and major events keep social tied to real fan moments.
  • Talent and freelancer oversight scales output without losing voice.
