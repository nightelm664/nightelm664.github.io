Partnerproof piece

Challenge

Sponsor reels during a Race to World First have to earn their place in a feed full of kills and memes. If the product feels bolted on, the community scrolls past — and the partner doesn’t get the association they paid for. Brief: integrate Corsair’s Scimitar Elite Wireless SE into the race story.

Approach

Partner reel framed around the race narrative — “ready to take down a snake” — so the hardware sits inside the same mythos as the bosses. Same visual language as organic RWF content; talent and environment already familiar to the audience.

Partner integration tied to the race narrative — useful as sponsor proof even when raw reach sits below kill posts.

Outcome

30.5K views, 251 likes on Instagram. Reach is lower than world-first or wrap posts (as expected for partner content), but the piece demonstrates clean brand integration inside a live competitive narrative — exactly what hiring managers and partners look for in a case study.

Why it worked

  • Product story locked to the race (“snake”) so it feels native, not interruptive.
  • Same production pipeline as organic RWF content — audience doesn’t feel a tone break.
  • Complements AMD day-in-life partner work as a second proof of sponsor delivery.
  • Honest metric framing: partner proof ≠ always top-of-chart views.
