About

Joe Cunnell

Creative community and marketing professional — gaming communities, marketing strategy, and content that understands the culture it speaks to. Based in the UK.

I’ve been in World of Warcraft since 2008. That isn’t a fun fact for the about page — it’s the reason the content works. I know what a world-first kill feels like from the community side, what nostalgia hits for returning players, and when a joke is for raiders versus for everyone who’s ever queued into a dungeon.

As Nightelm I create and stream; as Social Media Manager for Echo Esports I lead social for Race to World First (25M+ views from CV, partners including AMD). Before that I was Social Media Specialist at GIANTX (formerly EXCEL) across LoL and VALORANT — day-to-day social, a Discord of 28,500 members, influencer coordination, and brand work with JD Sports, EE, Just Eat and HSBC.

Earlier, at Meltdown Gaming Bars, I ran weekly activations across Valorant, CS:GO, Overwatch and fighting games, plus a WoW Dragonflight announcement campaign. Live events have included Gamescom, Insomnia and Dreamhack.

I care about putting gaming audiences first — honest metrics, culture-native voice, and campaigns that still feel like they belong in the feed.
